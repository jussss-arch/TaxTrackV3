💡 About TaxTrack

🎯 Purpose

TaxTrack is a tax monitoring and compliance assistance tool designed for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs). It helps business owners organize tax-related information, monitor filing deadlines, and improve compliance through automated reminders and tracking features.

👁 Vision

To make tax compliance simpler, more accessible, and less overwhelming for small business owners.

🚀 Mission

To provide a user-friendly platform that helps businesses stay informed about tax obligations, monitor important deadlines, and reduce missed filings.

👥 Intended Users

  • Micro Enterprises (MSEs)
  • Small Enterprises (SEs)
  • Sole Proprietors
  • Partnerships
  • Start-up Businesses

💡 Why TaxTrack?

Many small businesses struggle to keep track of filing deadlines, tax requirements, and compliance obligations. TaxTrack aims to reduce missed deadlines and improve tax awareness by providing a centralized monitoring platform.

⚖ Disclaimer

TaxTrack provides estimates, reminders, and informational assistance only. The application does not file tax returns, process tax payments, or replace professional tax advice. Users should verify all tax obligations with official BIR regulations and authorized tax professionals.
